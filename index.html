Wealth journey survey, team brief. Input to Aura Phase 1, October 2026.

What does the wealth management journey look like for people today?

Regardless of who they turn to: a bank, an AI assistant, an adviser, or services like Nordnet and Revolut.

Why we are asking

OP’s 2028 vision describes a coaching-led journey, but we know little about how people actually move through it today. What we do know is that it doesn’t happen in one place:

  • 28% of customers already invest with another provider (Inspiration deck, August 2026).
  • Customers already use public AI tools to support investment decisions (Wealth & AI Tribe alignment, September 2026).
  • Customers in the same tier describe the same advice as excellent or as basic, and some miss being contacted (Customer Experience Review H1 2026).

What we want to learn at each step

The steps follow the Accenture journey, which lines up with OP’s 2028 vision. For each step, people tell us who or what they used in the past year, wherever that help came from, and why. Everyone also tells us where they would most like more help.

StepWhat we want to learn

What we want the survey to surface

A map of the journeyFor each of the six steps, how many people use a bank, another platform, AI, an adviser, search and social media, family, or no one.
Why people go where they go, and where they want more helpThe reasons people choose one source over another, and the steps where needs are missed.
Needs groupsThree to five groups of people defined by how they think about money. We also check whether these groups follow balance levels or cut across them.
Long term and readinessWhat risk means to people, how far ahead they plan, what they know about retirement, what happens when goals change, and how ready they are.
Relationship and AIFamily conversations about wealth, how people see all their money together, when they check AI answers with a person, and what keeps them with one provider.
